## 📐 Design Language Summary

```
Theme:       Dark (Black-dominant, high contrast)
Accent:      Orange-Red (#EC460B) — aggressive, energetic CTA
Typography:  SVN-Gilroy (primary) + Be Vietnam Pro (secondary)
Style:       Editorial / Event / Community platform
Social Icons: Twitch, Discord, Facebook, Spotify, TikTok, Reddit, Figma
Content:     Vietnamese & English bilingual
Event Venue: Park Hyatt Saigon (luxury positioning)
```
